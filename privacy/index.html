Mitohoh

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mitohoh iPhone app (bundle ID com.mitohoh.ios) handles information. Mitohoh is an on-device beauty studio that helps you try makeup shades on a portrait you capture or import, keep formula notes, and freeze a before-and-after PNG compare card.

By using Mitohoh, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the app and remove it from your device.

1. Summary

  • Mitohoh does not require you to create an account.
  • Portraits, shade formulas, notes, compare cards, and Look balance are stored in the app sandbox on your iPhone.
  • This version does not operate a Mitohoh backend that uploads your portraits or notes for cloud storage.
  • Camera, Photos, Microphone, speech recognition, notifications, and tracking prompts appear only for the features that need them.
  • Look packs are purchased through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use Mitohoh, the app may store the following locally:

  • Portrait media you capture with the camera or import from Photos for shade try-on.
  • Beauty records such as active zone, selected shades, coverage intensity, lighting profile, and draft status.
  • Formula notes you type or dictate.
  • Finished compare cards as PNG images generated on device.
  • Look balance and purchase ledger used to grant and deduct Look packs after Apple-verified transactions.
  • Permission and onboarding choices needed to remember that you completed first-run screens.

This information stays on your device unless you choose to export or save a compare card into Photos, or unless you clear or delete app data.

3. Information we do not collect for a Mitohoh account

Mitohoh does not ask for a username, password, phone number, or profile to use the core studio features. This version does not sync your lookbook to a Mitohoh cloud account.

4. Permissions and why they are requested

Camera. Used to capture a portrait photo for makeup try-on and landmark-assisted preview. The camera is not used in the background when you are not capturing.

Photos. Used when you choose an existing portrait from your library, and when you ask Mitohoh to save a finished compare card into Photos. Access is limited to the actions you start.

Microphone and speech recognition. Used only when you dictate makeup-look notes. Speech is converted into text for the note field on your device. If you refuse these permissions, you can still type notes manually.

Notifications. May be requested so the system can deliver optional alerts related to the app. Mitohoh does not replace the system notification delegate to intercept unrelated notifications.

App Tracking Transparency. iOS may ask whether Mitohoh may use the advertising identifier. Your choice is stored by Apple’s system. This version does not send that identifier to a third-party tracking or advertising network for cross-app tracking.

You can refuse any permission prompt and continue using features that do not need that permission. You can also change permissions later in iOS Settings.

5. On-device processing

Shade try-on, face landmark assistance, compare-card composition, and Look accounting are performed on your iPhone. Visual results are a creative reference for your own use and are not medical, dermatological, or professional cosmetic advice.

6. Purchases and Apple services

Look packs are consumable In-App Purchases sold through the App Store. When you buy a pack:

  • Apple processes payment and provides a verified transaction.
  • Mitohoh records the verified transaction ID and granted Looks in the local purchase ledger so Looks are not granted twice for the same transaction.
  • Apple may receive information required to complete the purchase under Apple’s own privacy policy and terms.

Mitohoh does not receive your full payment card number. Refund requests, if any, are handled by Apple according to App Store rules.

7. Children

Mitohoh is intended for a general audience capable of managing device permissions and purchases. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for a Mitohoh account.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete individual records, use Clear All Data inside the app, delete the app, or reset your device in a way that removes the app sandbox. Clearing All Data permanently removes beauty records, finished compare cards, drafts, media stored by the app, and unused Looks on that device. Unused Looks cannot be recovered afterwards.

9. Security

Mitohoh relies on iOS sandbox storage and Apple’s purchase verification for transaction integrity. No method of storage is perfectly secure. Protect your device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID, and keep iOS updated.

10. Third-party services

Purchases and system permission dialogs are provided by Apple. Opening this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Use inside the app uses a web view to load the published pages. Those pages are hosted for legal disclosure and do not require you to create a Mitohoh account.

11. International use

Mitohoh is distributed through the App Store. Processing described in this policy primarily occurs on your device and through Apple’s purchase systems in the regions where Apple operates.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, permissions, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we publish a revision. Continued use of Mitohoh after an update means you accept the revised policy.
