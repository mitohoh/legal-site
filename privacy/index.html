Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Mitohoh is an on-device iPhone app for trying makeup shades on a portrait you capture or import. This policy describes information handled by the current version of Mitohoh (bundle ID com.mitohoh.ios).

Information stored on your device

Portraits, shade formulas, notes, compare cards, and Look balance are saved in the app sandbox on your iPhone. Mitohoh does not create an account and does not operate a Mitohoh server for this data.

Permissions

Camera is used to capture a portrait photo for shade try-on. Photos access is used to choose a portrait and, if you ask, to save a finished compare card. The microphone is used only when you dictate makeup look notes. Speech recognition transcribes that speech into notes. You can refuse any of these requests and keep using other features.

Tracking

iOS may ask whether Mitohoh may access the advertising identifier for measurement. This version does not send that identifier to a tracking service. Your choice is stored by the system.

Purchases

Look packs are purchased through Apple. Apple processes payment. Mitohoh records the resulting Look balance on this device so a compare card can be frozen.
